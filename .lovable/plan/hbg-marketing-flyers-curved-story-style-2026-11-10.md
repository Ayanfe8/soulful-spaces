# HBG marketing flyers (curved story style)

## What you get
Three tall phone-size flyers (1080x1920, for Instagram and WhatsApp status), one per core service, following the layout of your first sample:
1. Interior Styling
2. Wellness-Inspired Living
3. Modern Heritage & Luxury Curation

## Look
- Bone-coloured background with a large curved photo frame holding a newly made room scene in HBG style (terracotta, clay, umber, natural textures, warm light).
- Large serif headline over the photo (Fraunces style), short service line underneath, and a "Book a consultation" pill button.
- Small stacked brand words in the top right, the real HBG logo in the top corner.
- A small note box in the lower right, and a strip at the bottom: STYLING | WELLNESS | HERITAGE.
- No offer or discount.

1. Make three new room scenes, one per service.

Scenes via image generation (no text in them); layout composed in Python/PIL with bundled fonts so the wording is exact; logo from src/assets/hbg-logo.webp; outputs as PNG in /mnt/documents/media/images/.
