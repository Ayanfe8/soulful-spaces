# Refine the shared site footer

## What will change
- Remove the shared “Ready to create a space that feels like you?” section completely, leaving the footer as the only shared closing block.
- Rework the footer into four equal responsive columns: Brand, Quick Links, Services, and Connect.
- Move service destinations out of Quick Links into their own vertical list: Styling, Wellness, and Heritage.
- Keep the mobile order Brand → Quick Links → Services → Connect, with each section full-width.
- Restore the footer’s general text to the site-wide sans font used by body copy and navigation; retain the serif only for the brand name, matching the site’s established heading treatment.
